# HAMDI TOURISM — Hamid Shah's operating guide

This guide describes the existing application **after approved database setup and
live acceptance**. Current Owner access must be verified privately on the actual serving host; this guide does not activate or change an account. No default
password, automatic Owner role or developer Admin account is provided.

## Start with a client-review session

Open the private Codespaces link using [Preview instructions](PREVIEW.md).
The homepage, marketing categories, login forms and honest unavailable states can
be reviewed now. Admin setup below requires the client's genuine authorized Owner
session. Do not promise booking, email, AI or tracking acceptance from this review.

First-time setup order: **Business Settings → Locations → Vehicles → Drivers →
Routes → Route pricing → Passenger booking configuration**. Use the navigation in
`/admin`; publish only genuine, client-approved records. Staff invitations send real
email and record creation changes production: this sprint did not authorize or
perform them. The client/operator must approve those actual setup operations.

## Sign in and protect access

Use `/login` on the approved HTTPS business domain. Your verified Owner account
opens `/admin`; Driver accounts open `/driver-account`, Passenger accounts open
`/passenger`. If access is pending, contact the authorized setup operator. Do not
register another account or share your password to work around it. Password recovery
uses the real email service and must be tested before handover. Use Sign out when
finished, especially on shared devices. Keep recovery email access secure.

## Set up the real business before accepting reservations

1. In **Business Settings**, enter approved contact/support details, description and
   booking/cancellation policies. Review the profile before publishing it. Enabling
   enquiries and enabling Passenger reservations are distinct controls.
2. In **Locations**, record real pickup/drop-off points and verified map coordinates.
3. In **Vehicles**, enter only approved actual inventory, status, real passenger and
   luggage limits, registration/expiry and authorized photographs. Marketing images
   are examples and never proof that a vehicle is available.
4. Keep the four service categories: Normal Sedan (3 passengers), Luxury Sedan
   (3 passengers), 7-Seater (7 seats), 5-Passenger Vehicle (5 passengers). Actual
   bookable vehicle limits must match its documentation; never infer them from a
   showcase image or category label.
5. In **Routes**, connect the real pickup/drop-off pair. In **Route pricing** and
   **Optional extras**, enter approved AED fares, charges and any real return fare.
   Publish only rates you intend customers to see.
6. Open **Business Settings → Passenger booking configuration**. Provide genuine
   route durations, privacy/terms and approved booking policies before enabling
   reservations. Prices, inventory, route capacity and timing are checked again
   when a request is submitted; a displayed quote is not an assignment guarantee.

If anything is missing, keep online booking unavailable and use published support
channels. Do not enter placeholder prices, coordinates, policies or vehicles.

## Drivers and staff

Create a real Driver profile with its actual email, contact and licence information.
Use the authorized Driver invitation action on that record; the recipient follows
the real email link and sets their own password. A Driver must have a confirmed
account, active membership and the matching approved Driver profile before accessing
assignments. An already registered account requires identity/access review, not a
second account or automatic promotion. Failed or uncertain invitations require
review of the staff/Driver list before retrying.

**Staff & access** manages existing permitted roles and deactivation. The Admin
application is restricted to the verified client Owner. Inviting another staff role
is not a way to create a second Admin/Owner. Owner identity/role changes require the
separate approved setup process. Deactivate access promptly when someone leaves.

## Bookings and assignments

Review **Transfer enquiries** and **Bookings**. A new Passenger request is pending;
it is not automatically a confirmed reservation or Driver assignment. Review the
real passenger/contact information, route, dates, seats/luggage and pricing.
Select a genuinely available, licensed Driver and eligible vehicle. Scheduling and
capacity conflicts are rejected; do not bypass those checks by editing unrelated
fields. Archive records through the supported controls instead of deleting history.

The assigned Driver uses Accept → Start journey → Arrive → Pick up → Complete,
as allowed by the actual trip state. Active reassignments follow the authorized
handover flow. After a network interruption, refresh before repeating a status change.
A Passenger who sees an uncertain booking response should first check **Your journeys**
or contact support; do not create a second request without checking the first.

## Live trips and tracking

Use **Live Operations** to view authorized active trips. Drivers must explicitly
consent before sharing their actual device location. They can stop sharing; ending
or revoking an assignment removes access. The browser currently stops sharing when
hidden/offline; continuous background tracking is not guaranteed on mobile browsers.
Keep the trip page open while safely operating the service. Never interact with a
phone while driving.

Passengers see only their authorized assigned trip. Missing, stale or revoked GPS
is reported honestly. Maps require connectivity; route/ETA estimates additionally
require the approved routing provider and fresh GPS. Estimates are not live traffic
or guaranteed arrival times. The decorative homepage road/car is not live tracking.

## Cancellation, revenue and support

Review Passenger cancellation requests before changing a real booking. A request
is not an automatic cancellation or refund. Follow the published policy and approved
payment process. Revenue reports use verified payment evidence; changing a booking
status does not mark a payment received. Never ask a customer for card credentials
through chat. Payment-provider setup, real charges and refunds require their own
approved acceptance process.

If a save fails, preserve the entered details, refresh the record and check whether
the update already happened. Query failures are not evidence that the business has
zero bookings. Contact the authorized support operator for account/database issues;
never disable security policies or share server keys to troubleshoot.
