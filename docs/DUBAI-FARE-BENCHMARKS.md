# Dubai transfer fare benchmark and owner review

Research snapshot: **18 October 2026**. The competitor examples below are
publicly displayed Transfeero prices, not Hamdi Tourism quotes, approved fares,
or a complete market survey. The ECB's last published EUR reference rate on
the research date was 16 October 2026: EUR 1 = USD 1.1253. Using the UAE
dirham's USD peg of AED 3.6725/USD gives an indicative conversion of
**EUR 1 ≈ AED 4.1327**. Rounded AED figures are comparisons only; exchange and
checkout prices can differ.

Transfeero's DXB landing page also advertises starting fares of EUR 17.34 for
Economy, EUR 28.90 for Standard Sedan and Standard Van, EUR 57.80 for First

competitor classes have matching vehicles or capacities.

### Route and policy observations

Transfeero's Burj Khalifa and Palm pages describe fixed prices that include
Salik; its DXB guidance advertises up to 60 minutes' free airport-arrival wait
and 15 minutes for other pickups. Its page describes Salik as AED 4–6 per
crossing with time/day-dependent rates and notes the free overnight interval.
These statements describe that competitor's offer, not Hamdi's. Confirm current
Salik fees and applicable gates against the official [Salik tariff
information](https://www.salik.ae/en/toll-gates) before configuring a local
policy. Competitor pages mention traffic variation, but no reliable public
night surcharge or luggage/oversize-bag supplement was established by this
research.

These "owner input" cells are deliberately not filled with fabricated business
fares. Use **Admin → Fare review** to enter prices only for existing routes and
vehicle categories supported by actual, non-archived fleet records. The workflow
saves drafts as unpublished. An owner must separately review the row in Route
pricing and explicitly publish it before public quotes can use it. Add no
imaginary destinations or fleet.

Before publication, cost the actual vehicle and route, decide whether Salik is
included or passed through, set after-hours service rules, airport vs
non-airport wait time and overtime fees, luggage/capacity limits, child-seat
availability, and hotel/resort access or parking fees. Keep manual
hotel/address/landmark details available and request a human quote for any
unsupported or unpriced journey. No paid map API is needed.
